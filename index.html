Tayka Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Tayka is a photo-a-day app: everyone in the world gets the same prompt at the same moment, and you can't see anyone else's answer until you post your own. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to reach us.

What we collect

  • Your identity, from Sign in with Apple. Tayka uses Sign in with Apple exclusively — there is no email/password account. Depending on the choice you made when signing in, we receive either your real email address or an Apple-generated private relay address, plus a stable identifier for your account. We never see your Apple password.
  • A username you choose. Shown next to everything you post.
  • The photos you post. One per day, tied to that day's prompt.
  • Captions and comments you or others write on posts.
  • Who you follow and who you've blocked. Following is one-way (like Instagram); it controls whose Tayks appear in your feed. Blocking hides someone from you and you from them, in both directions, and also removes any follow between you.
  • Top Tayk votes. If you opt a post into Top Tayk, other users may see it and vote on it; we record which vote you cast and which four photos you were shown, so a fair win rate can be calculated.
  • A phone number (optional). You may choose to add a phone number so friends can find your profile by searching for it. The number is verified by SMS code and stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash — the raw number is never stored and cannot be reconstructed. You can remove it at any time from Settings. Adding a phone number is entirely optional.

What we don't collect

Tayka has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. There is no email/password login, so we never see or store a password. We don't collect precise location, contacts, or anything from outside the app itself. We never read your address book or contacts list.

Where your data lives

Tayka's backend runs on Supabase, hosted in the US East region. Photos, posts, comments, and account data are all stored there, protected by row-level security rules that determine exactly who is allowed to read what — for example, a day's photos are invisible to you until you've posted your own that day, and blocked users cannot see each other's content regardless of who follows whom.

Who can see what you post

Your photo and caption for a given day are visible only to people who (a) follow you and (b) have posted their own photo for that day. If you opt a post into "Top Tayk," a wider audience may see and vote on it. Comments on a post are visible to whoever can see that post.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. This permanently removes your photos, posts, comments, follows, blocks, and account record. It cannot be undone.

Reporting content or contacting us

If you see something on Tayka that shouldn't be there — abusive content, harassment, or anything else — you can report it directly from the app (on a photo or a comment), or reach us by email. We review every report and respond within 48 hours.

Email: tayka@att.net

Blocking

Blocking someone in Tayka removes any follow relationship between you and that person in both directions, and hides their content from you and yours from them — including in Top Tayk. You can review and undo your blocks at any time in Settings.

Children

Tayka is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: tayka@att.net.
